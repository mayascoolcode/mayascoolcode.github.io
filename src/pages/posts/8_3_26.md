Hi.

I'm Mortalmutt999. 

As a kid, I spent a lot of time on the internet playing GirlsGoGames and watching Monster High webisodes. I posted "cringe" aesthetic photo edits I made in Picsart on Instagram and read yaoi fanfiction on the Wii Internet Channel. I drew ponies because I had a horse-girl friend in elementry school. Years later, I am sitting on my bed with my plushies and Shigaraki body pillow, making websites. 

The MMDungeon concept was created back around 2020, as I learned HTML/CSS. I wanted my own personal website to host all my projects and thoughts, away from traditional social media. Mostly inspired by the old web and communities like Neocities. So I began to create. Back then, I went under numerous monikers that I can barely remember now until settling on this one. Struggling with creative burnout also kept me from finishing much, and most of that era was lost to time.

Now, I am a computer science major transfering to univerity this month. I'm going though big changes and many parts of my life won't be the same anymore. I am moving out, getting a job, and managing relationships on my own. I'm excited and can't wait for it, and I want to revive this website as my outlet to document that journey and the things I make. My  new "digital time capsule" to look back on as I live. A long term project I can work on as I change.

It will be updated on the monthly.

I think it will be fun. ^_^
